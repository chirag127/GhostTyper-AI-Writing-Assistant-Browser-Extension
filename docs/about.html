About GhostTyper

GhostTyper brings Copilot-style inline ghost-text suggestions to every text field in your browser. Type a few words, see a suggestion in grey — press Tab or Right Arrow to accept, or keep typing to dismiss.

Philosophy

  • Client-side only — no backend, no telemetry
  • Privacy first — sensitive fields are never read
  • Zero-config default via Pollinations (free, no key)
  • Open source, MIT licensed
